### 🚩 Past Events

## Capture The Flag (CTF) – January 2026

**OWASP Vignan University Student Chapter** successfully organized a Capture The Flag (CTF) competition at the Department of ACSE.

**📅 Date:** January 29–31, 2026  
**🏫 Venue:** Vignan University – ACSE  
**🏆 Prize Pool:** ₹6000  
**👥 Organizer:** CLAW (Cyber Club at Vignan)

### Event Highlights
- Practical cybersecurity challenges
- Hands-on vulnerability exploitation
- Team-based competition format
- Online registration and challenge portal

The event witnessed enthusiastic participation from students and helped strengthen practical cybersecurity skills across multiple domains including web security, cryptography, and system exploitation.
